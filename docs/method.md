Handling: Unclassified — public

# Method — reading a cell

**A cell** is one layer crossed with one sovereign capability, for example `SDL-M7` (Sovereign Decision Layer × Space).

**A reading** answers one question for that cell, through a stated lens: *should a middle power build, partner or buy here, and why?*

| Posture | Read it as |
|---|---|
| BUILD | Control is the point. Held and operated under national jurisdiction. |
| PARTNER | Plurality is the point. Secured with allies across several jurisdictions, never a single dependency. |
| BUY | Markets work. Procured competitively, with sovereignty and data conditions in the contract. |
| UNREAD | Not yet read. A gap in coverage, not a finding. |

## Two tests worth applying

1. **Jurisdiction, not residency.** A server or plant inside the country does not settle who can compel access to it. Ask who has legal reach.
2. **Must it be sovereign, and can a foreign balance sheet buy it?** If the answer to the first is no, it is rarely BUILD. If the answer to the second is yes, ownership conditions belong in the reading.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| confirmed | Primary source: statute, filing, official publication |
| reported | Credible secondary source |
| inferred | Analytic judgement, stated as such |
| speculative | A hypothesis worth testing |
| no_evidence | Searched, nothing found. That is a finding, not a blank. |

## What a reading never contains

Dollar figures, personal information, confidential or export-controlled information, or unpublished third-party analysis.
